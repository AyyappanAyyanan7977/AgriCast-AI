# Demonstration agriculture notes (illustrative, not authoritative)

This bundled note exists to demonstrate local retrieval in AgriCast AI. It is not an official agricultural recommendation. Crop yield depends on crop variety, soil properties, rainfall timing, temperature, pests, irrigation, nutrient management, and local conditions.

Rainfall totals alone are insufficient to make a reliable yield forecast: distribution across crop growth stages and drainage conditions can matter. Temperature effects vary by crop, variety, and growth stage.

Before applying any agronomic recommendation, consult a qualified local agricultural extension officer and verify recommendations against official, region-specific sources. Treat correlations in a dataset as associations rather than evidence of causation.
